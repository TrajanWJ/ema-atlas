## How This Section Works

Add notes here for people who come up repeatedly in your work. Each person note should capture:

- **Role / relationship** — colleague, client, collaborator, mentor
- **What they own** — "handles infra," "runs the design team"
- **Working style** — preferences, communication style, timezone
- **Context Claude needs** — "prefers detailed PRs," "allergic to over-engineering"

### Note format

```markdown
---
date: YYYY-MM-DD
tags:
  - people
  - [role]
---

# [Name]

**Role:** [What they do / relationship to you]
**Context:** [What Claude needs to know to work well in their context]

## Notes

[Anything else relevant — preferences, projects, history]
```

### Privacy

- Keep entries factual and work-relevant
- No sensitive personal information
- Focus on context that helps Claude collaborate better
