1. **Wait it out** — primary has 5.4 hours left, guardian is running, 429 will clear well before then. Guardian will auto-refresh both tokens when the rate limit lifts. This is the zero-risk path.

3. **Just use one account** — the backup was a nice-to-have failover. With LCM and smarter session management, you burn less tokens anyway.

I'd go with option 1. The system is working fine on the primary right now.
