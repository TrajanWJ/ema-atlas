---
title: Roles
description: How clients, talent, and admins interact inside Autharis.
---

Autharis has three first-class roles. Each role sees a distinct product
surface, but they share a common underlying data model — talent, job
requests, engagements, timesheets, invoices, and admin-queue items.

## Client

Clients post **job requests**, review matched talent, start **engagements**,
approve **timesheets**, and pay **invoices**.

Primary surface: `/client` (and labs at `/client-request`, `/client-match`,
`/client-finance`).

Typical flow:

1. Draft a brief in the request composer.
2. Review the ranked match list (see the [matching model](/guides/matching/)).
3. Open an engagement with the chosen talent.
4. Approve submitted timesheets weekly.
5. Settle invoices as they are issued (see [payments](/guides/payments/)).

## Talent

Talent maintain a **profile**, browse **opportunities**, accept engagements,
submit **timesheets**, and watch payouts land on their **earnings ledger**.

Primary surface: `/talent` (with deep dives at
`/talent/opportunities/[id]`, `/talent/earnings/ledger`, and a public profile
lab at `/talent-profile`).

Typical flow:

1. Keep profile skills, industries, and availability current.
2. Apply to opportunities surfaced by the matcher.
3. Log hours into the weekly timesheet.
4. Track invoices as they progress from issued → paid → paid-out.

## Admin

Admins run the **activation queue**, keep the talent roster healthy, audit
**matching runs**, handle **disputes**, and publish **weekly reports**.

Primary surface: `/admin` (with drilldowns at `/admin/queue/[id]`,
`/admin/matching/[runId]`, `/admin/disputes/[caseId]`, and
`/admin/reports/weekly`).

Admins never act on behalf of users silently — every override is logged in
the audit trail and visible in the weekly workbook.
