# Design Inspiration — Web Design Galleries

A curated catalog of niche web design inspiration galleries. Each focuses on a specific slice of web design rather than trying to be another generic Dribbble/Behance. Reviewed March 2026.

---

## 1. Curated Design (curated.design)

**URL:** https://curated.design → redirects to https://craftwork.design/curated/websites/
**What it is:** A curated web design inspiration catalog. Originally an independent site, now absorbed into Craftwork Design (a design asset/illustration studio). Functions as a gallery of handpicked website designs.
**Who runs it:** Craftwork Design Studio (craftwork.design) — a design product company that sells UI kits, illustrations, and design assets.
**Pricing:** Free to browse. The parent site (Craftwork) sells design products and subscription plans.

### Organization
- Category-based filtering (SaaS, Agency, Portfolio, E-commerce, etc.)
- Visual grid layout with screenshots
- Submit your own site for review
- Advertising/promotion slots available

### Volume
- Heavily JS-rendered (React/Next.js app) — content doesn't render well without full browser
- Appeared to have hundreds of entries based on Wayback Machine captures
- Updated periodically but unclear cadence

### Features
- Submission form for community contributions
- Advertisement page for promoting products
- Clean visual presentation with large screenshots
- Category filtering

### Quality Assessment
- **Medium-high quality** — handpicked, not auto-scraped
- The acquisition by Craftwork is notable — this was originally an independent project and the redirect suggests it may have been absorbed/acqui-hired
- Content is genuinely curated (they review submissions against "quality standards")
- Heavy JS rendering makes it hard to browse without a modern browser
- Feels more like a marketing funnel for Craftwork products than a pure community resource now

### Competitors/Related
- [[Awwwards]], [[Siteinspire]], [[Godly]], [[Mobbin]]
- Craftwork itself sells design assets, so this serves as top-of-funnel

## 2. Landing.love

**URL:** https://landing.love (https://www.landing.love/)
**What it is:** A showcase of the best animation-focused websites and landing pages, with full-page video recordings capturing scroll interactions, animations, and transitions.
**Who runs it:** Independent project, partnered with Lapa.Ninja, UIStore.Design, and Bookmarks.Design. Contact via hello@landing.love.
**Pricing:** 100% free. Always accessible.

### Organization
- **48+ categories** organized by industry and style
- Categories include: Business, Illustration, SaaS, E-commerce, Agency, Portfolio, etc.
- Each entry has: site name, description, category tags, link to original site, prev/next navigation
- Browsable by category at /categories/

### Volume
- **1,963+ curated websites** (as of March 2026)
- Regularly updated with new designs
- Significant depth — not just a handful of entries

### Features
- **Full-page video recordings** — this is the killer feature. Captures animations, micro-interactions, scroll effects, and transitions that static screenshots miss
- RSS feed available (/index.xml)
- Submit sites via email
- Category browsing
- Partner links to learn design (Webflow University, Framer docs)

### Quality Assessment
- **High quality** — genuinely useful and differentiated
- The video recordings are a real standout — most competitors only show static screenshots
- Nearly 2,000 entries with 48+ categories shows serious curation effort
- Free with no paywall or freemium upsell
- Clean, focused UI without clutter
- Good for seeing *how* a site actually feels, not just how it looks
- Excellent for animation/interaction inspiration specifically

### Standout Patterns
- Heavy emphasis on animation and motion design
- Sites tagged with both industry (Business, SaaS) and style (Illustration, Minimal)
- Prev/next navigation encourages browsing flow

### Competitors/Related
- Partners: [[Lapa.Ninja]] (landing page inspiration), [[UIStore.Design]] (free design resources), [[Bookmarks.Design]] (design resource directory)
- Tool affiliates: Webflow, Framer, Screen Studio
- Competes with: [[Godly]], [[Awwwards]], [[Land-book]]

## 3. Saaspo (saaspo.com)

**URL:** https://saaspo.com
**What it is:** A curated collection of the best SaaS website designs, organized by page type, industry, style, assets, and tech stack. Focused exclusively on SaaS landing pages and marketing sites.
**Who runs it:** Independent project, Twitter: @saaspo_
**Pricing:** Free to browse. Weekly newsletter. Accepts advertising/sponsorship.

### Organization
- **Page Types filter:** Landing pages (1,173 examples), Pricing pages (469 examples), and likely more categories (about, features, blog, etc.)
- **Industry filter:** Filters by SaaS vertical
- **Style filter:** Visual style categorization
- **Assets filter:** Type of visual assets used
- **Stack filter:** Filter by tech stack (Webflow, Framer, etc.)

### Volume
- **1,173+ landing page examples** alone
- **469+ pricing page examples**
- Total likely 1,500-2,000+ entries across all page types
- Updated daily (per their claim)

### Features
- Multi-dimensional filtering (page type, industry, style, assets, stack) — very powerful
- Weekly newsletter ("Get the 7 best SaaS designs in your inbox each week")
- 2,258+ newsletter subscribers
- Submit form for community contributions
- FAQ section with educational content
- Sponsored slots available

### Quality Assessment
- **High quality** — this is a serious, well-maintained resource
- The multi-filter system is the standout feature — being able to filter by page type AND industry AND style is powerful
- SaaS-only focus means every entry is relevant if you're building a SaaS product
- Large volume (1,000+ entries) with daily updates
- Currently behind Cloudflare protection (403'd our fetch) — may be experiencing bot traffic issues or intentionally blocking scrapers
- Newsletter with 2,000+ subscribers suggests real community
- Sponsors include Framer, Mobbin, Shadcn Blocks, Two Hands Studio — quality sponsors indicate quality audience

### Standout Patterns
- Page-type filtering is brilliant — most galleries show only homepages, but being able to browse pricing pages, feature pages, etc. separately is incredibly useful for designers working on specific sections
- Tech stack filtering lets you find inspiration in your own platform (e.g., "show me the best Framer SaaS sites")
- Clean FAQ with educational content shows they care about the audience, not just volume

### Competitors/Related
- Sponsors/links: [[Framer]], [[Mobbin]], [[Shadcn Blocks]], Two Hands Studio
- Competes with: [[SaaS Landing Page]] examples on Landingfolio, [[SaaSFrame]], [[Page Collective]]

## 4. Navbar Gallery (navbar.gallery)

**URL:** https://navbar.gallery (https://www.navbar.gallery/)
**What it is:** A curated navigation-focused design inspiration gallery. Exclusively showcases navbar/navigation patterns from real websites.
**Who runs it:** Christina Liubynska (https://www.supercharged.design/), built in public on Twitter/X (@Suupercharged)
**Pricing:** Free. Sponsored by Mobbin.

### Organization
- **8 navigation types:**
  1. **Static/Sticky Navbar** — simple fixed navigation bars
  2. **Dropdown/Flyout Menu** — nested menu items in parent items
  3. **Mega Menu** — large dropdowns with multiple categories
  4. **Side Bar** — vertical panel navigation
  5. **Search Bar** — input fields for search
  6. **Announcement Bar** — slim banners for promotions/updates
  7. **Full Screen Menu** — full-page menus with graphic elements
  8. **Breadcrumbs** — secondary navigation for location tracing
- Browse all at /browse

### Volume
- Moderate collection — focused niche means fewer entries than general galleries
- Growing collection with community submissions

### Features
- Community submission form (share your favorite navbar + Twitter handle for credit)
- Clean categorization by navbar type
- Simple, focused browsing experience
- No search or filtering beyond type categories

### Quality Assessment
- **High quality for its niche** — fills a genuine gap
- Christina's origin story is authentic: she saw footer.design existed and realized there was no equivalent for navigation
- Built in public on Twitter — transparent development
- Mobbin sponsorship lends credibility
- Very narrow focus (just navbars) means it's either exactly what you need or irrelevant
- Good for designers specifically working on navigation patterns
- Could benefit from more metadata per entry (tech stack, responsive behavior, accessibility notes)

### Standout Patterns
- The categorization is smart — breaking navbars into 8 distinct types helps you find the right pattern
- Announcement bars and breadcrumbs as separate categories is a nice touch — these are often overlooked
- Mega Menu vs Dropdown distinction is useful for information architecture decisions

### Competitors/Related
- Inspired by: [[footer.design]] (footer design patterns)
- Sponsored by: [[Mobbin]] (design pattern library)
- Created by: Supercharged Design (Christina's studio)
- Sister niche sites: [[footer.design]], [[cta.gallery]], [[hero.gallery]]

## 5. CTA Gallery (cta.gallery)

**URL:** https://cta.gallery (https://www.cta.gallery/)
**What it is:** A curated gallery of call-to-action (CTA) designs from real websites, focused on inspiring creativity and maximizing conversions.
**Who runs it:** Salim Lunat, founder of Webestica (template studio) and Iconstica (icon library). 8+ years of web design experience.
**Pricing:** Free. Sponsored by Webestica, Iconstica, and Framer templates.

### Organization
- **By CTA Type (Category):**
  - All Types
  - Button
  - Call-to-Buy
  - Download
  - Form
  - Modal / Pop-up
  - Navigation
  - Newsletter
  - Pricing / Subscription
- **By Industry:**
  - AI, SaaS, E-commerce, Agency, etc.
  - SaaS industry alone has 70+ entries (Langchain, Shopify, Webflow, Cursor, Ramp, Attio, etc.)
- **By Mode:**
  - Dark / Light mode variants

### Volume
- **~55-70+ unique CTA examples** visible on the main page (counting unique entries)
- SaaS industry category alone has 70+ entries
- Total likely 150-200+ across all categories
- Growing collection with submissions
- Each entry includes desktop AND mobile view screenshots

### Features
- **Desktop + Mobile views** for each CTA — shows responsive design
- Category, Industry, and Mode filtering
- Individual CTA detail pages with related CTAs
- Community submission form (Site URL, Designer, Submitter info)
- **CTA Writing Tips page** — educational content with good/bad examples covering:
  - Be Clear and Direct
  - Action-Oriented Verbs
  - Highlight Benefits
  - Personal Language
  - Create Urgency
  - Keep It Short
  - Remove Risk / Address Objections
  - Social Proof in CTA Text
  - A/B Testing advice
- Subscribe to newsletter
- Free Framer templates section

### Quality Assessment
- **High quality** — genuinely useful, well-organized, and educational
- The CTA writing tips page is excellent — not just showing designs but teaching principles
- Desktop + mobile views for each entry is a valuable differentiator
- Multi-dimensional filtering (type × industry × mode) is powerful
- Founder has real credentials (8 years, runs template businesses)
- The industry categorization (SaaS, AI, etc.) makes it practical for specific projects
- Notable featured sites: Langchain, Shopify, Webflow, Cursor, Ramp, Neon, ClickUp, Kinsta — these are real, high-quality companies
- Not spam — every entry links to a real website

### Standout Patterns
- The CTA type taxonomy is well-thought-out (distinguishing Button vs Form vs Modal vs Newsletter CTAs)
- Dark/Light mode filtering is practical — you often need CTA inspiration matching your site's theme
- Writing tips bridge the gap between visual inspiration and copywriting
- Good mix of well-known brands (Shopify, Webflow) and indie/startup sites

### Competitors/Related
- Inspired by: [[footer.design]]
- Created by: Salim Lunat / [[Webestica]] / [[Iconstica]]
- Sponsor ecosystem: Webestica templates (Webflow, Framer, Bootstrap)
- Sister niche sites: [[navbar.gallery]], [[footer.design]], [[hero.gallery]]

## Cross-Site Comparison

| Site | Focus | Volume | Free? | Killer Feature | Quality |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| curated.design | General web design | Hundreds | Yes (Craftwork upsell) | Craftwork ecosystem | ⭐⭐⭐ |
| landing.love | Animation websites | 1,963+ | 100% free | Full-page video recordings | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| saaspo.com | SaaS websites | 1,500+ | Yes (newsletter) | Multi-filter by page type/industry/stack | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| navbar.gallery | Navigation patterns | Moderate | Yes (Mobbin sponsor) | 8-type navbar taxonomy | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| cta.gallery | CTAs / Conversion | 150-200+ | Yes (sponsors) | Desktop+mobile views + writing tips | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |

### Recommendations
- **Best overall value:** landing.love — huge catalog, totally free, video recordings are unique
- **Best for SaaS builders:** saaspo.com — nothing else does page-type filtering this well
- **Best for specific components:** navbar.gallery + cta.gallery — narrow focus = deep relevance
- **Most interesting ecosystem play:** curated.design/Craftwork — design inspiration as marketing funnel

### The Niche Gallery Trend
All five sites share DNA: inspired by the success of component-specific galleries (footer.design being cited by multiple founders). The pattern is clear:
1. Pick one overlooked web component (navbars, CTAs, footers, heroes)
2. Curate 50-200 high-quality examples
3. Categorize thoughtfully
4. Monetize via sponsors + newsletter
5. Build in public on Twitter/X

This is a healthy ecosystem — much more useful than generic "top 100 websites" lists because the narrow focus means every entry is relevant when you need it.
