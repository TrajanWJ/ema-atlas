# Tiny Productivity / Utility vApps — Cross-Pollination Seed

## Why this area matters
EMA needs strong flagship surfaces and strong tiny wins. Small, well-shaped vApps can compound operator usefulness.

## Example targets from the catalog
- Notes
- Tasks
- Schedule / Calendar
- Brain Dumps
- Journal / Log
- Notifications Hub
- small utility/helper surfaces not yet canonized

## Relevant source seeds

### Memos
- Fast self-hosted capture.
- Potential imports:
  - low-friction note capture
  - quick-save / quick-review UX

### ntfy
- Lightweight notifications.
- Potential imports:
  - dead-simple event routing
  - quiet-hours / escalation concepts

### Dockge
- Reactive operational control UX.
- Potential imports:
  - confident, alive-feeling status panels
  - direct manipulation of runtime state

### Nezha
- Machine/system truth.
- Potential imports:
  - compact host/fleet truth widgets
  - small system-health views

## Questions to pursue
- Which tiny surfaces deserve their own vApps vs becoming widgets/cards?
- How should micro-productivity surfaces connect into the larger EMA intent/proposal/execution model?
