# Reasoning Traces Shape Outputs but Models Won't Say So

## Summary

arXiv:2603.20620v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Can we trust the reasoning traces that large reasoning models (LRMs) produce? We investigate whether these traces faithfully reflect what drives model outputs, and whether models will honestly report their influence. We introduce Thought Injection, a method that injects synthetic reasoning snippets into a model's trace, then measures whether the model follows the injected reasoning and acknowledges doing so.
