# Competitive Intel Scan — 2026-03-27

*Sources: 5 total (2 T1/T2 fetched, 3 T3 search context)*
*Confidence: Medium-High | Scope: Weekly highlights*

## Summary

Two major signals this week: OpenAI announced a strategic consolidation of its three products into a single superapp, explicitly motivated by Anthropic's competitive success. Simultaneously, Ramp's AI Index showed Anthropic achieving its largest-ever monthly adoption gain while OpenAI experienced its first-ever monthly decline. The narrative is directionally consistent: Anthropic is winning enterprise, OpenAI is reacting.

## Findings

### OpenAI Superapp Merger (March 20, 2026)

OpenAI will merge ChatGPT, Codex (coding platform), and Atlas (browser) into a single desktop superapp. Greg Brockman is overseeing consolidation; Fidji Simo handles distribution. Executive framing called Anthropic's bundled Claude suite a "wake-up call." The previous multi-product sprawl created "organizational fragmentation."

Strategic pivot: narrowing focus to coding tools and enterprise. Superapp will emphasize agentic, autonomous capabilities.

Source: [T2] The Decoder citing WSJ — https://the-decoder.com/openai-plans-to-merge-chatgpt-codex-and-atlas-browser-into-a-single-desktop-superapp/

### Ramp AI Index — February 2026 Data

Hard payment data (not survey):
- Anthropic: 24.4% business adoption, +4.9% MoM (record), 70% head-to-head win rate vs OpenAI among first-timers
- OpenAI: -1.5% adoption (first-ever single-month decline of this magnitude)
- Overall business AI adoption: 47.6% (record high)

Source: [T1] Ramp payment data — https://ramp.com/velocity/ai-index-march-2026

### Landscape Context (T3 corroboration)

- LangGraph now dominant for enterprise production agents; CrewAI strong for prototyping; AutoGen/AG2 slowing
- A2A protocol support: CrewAI added it, LangGraph/AutoGen lack native support
- NVIDIA launched Open Agent Development Platform with 15+ enterprise partners
- Cisco DefenseClaw: open-source AI agent security framework (RSA 2026)
- Cursor crossed 1M paying developers, launched parallel subagents

## Key Takeaways

1. **OpenAI is in reactive mode** — the superapp consolidation is a defensive move, not a confident product strategy
2. **Anthropic's integrated approach is proving out** — 70% new-customer win rate is a decisive signal
3. **Agent security becoming a real market** — Cisco DefenseClaw, NVIDIA's toolkit with security focus, NIST standards initiative all pointing same direction
