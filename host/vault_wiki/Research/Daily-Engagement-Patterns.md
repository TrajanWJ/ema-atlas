# What Makes People Actually Use Apps Daily

From analysis of 20+ competing products (Daylio, Notion AI, Tana, Anytype, Reflect, Habitica, Exist.io, Granola, Rewind).

## The Weekly Engagement Formula

### 1. 2-Minute Daily Micro-Entry
- Target: <30 seconds for check-in (Daylio: 2 taps)
- Show streak immediately (visual, not numeric)
- Push notification at optimal time (morning + evening)

### 2. Visible Weekly Ritual
- 20-30 min review (proposals reviewed, tasks done, gaps closed)
- Automatic insights: 'You killed 3 ideas with this pattern'
- Progress visualization over time

### 3. Ambient Capture (Zero Friction)
- Meeting transcription (Granola pattern)
- Global clipper (Tana pattern)
- Voice capture (Tana iOS voice chat)
- Feed Brain Dump automatically

### 4. AI as Visible Colleague
- Not 'ask Claude' — 'here's what your agents discovered'
- Proactive proposals, gap warnings, pattern crystallizations
- Trust built by seeing unsupervised work succeed

### 5. Privacy as Selling Point
- 'Your ideas never leave your machine'
- Local Claude CLI = differentiator vs Notion/Tana cloud

## Top Engagement Patterns by Evidence

| Pattern | Winner | Why |
|---------|--------|-----|
| 2-tap entry | Daylio | Friction is the killer |
| Visual streaks | Daylio/Way of Life | Gamify consistency |
| Auto correlations | Exist.io | Data becomes intelligence |
| Ambient capture | Rewind/Granola | Active entry is friction |
| MCP-first design | Anytype/Obsidian | AI feels native |
| Meeting-time utility | Tana/Granola | Value at highest leverage moment |
| Visible autonomy | Notion 20-min agents | Trust through results |

## What NO Competitor Does Well (EMA's Opportunity)
1. Intent-to-execution traceability
2. Multi-modality without chaos (task + note + decision + meeting)
3. AI as colleague, not tool (agents that initiate)
4. Weekly rhythm without monthly fatigue
5. Learning from patterns across 1000+ items
