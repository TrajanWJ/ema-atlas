# Workstream Identity (Draft)

## Principle
A workstream is the durable unit of active human↔agent work across all surfaces.

## It must survive across
- GUI
- CLI
- Discord/chat mirrors
- terminal/live sessions
- chronicle/replay
- review/approval surfaces

## Workstream should link
- intention(s)
- proposal(s)
- approval event(s)
- execution(s)
- trace(s)
- scratchpad(s)
- comms thread(s)
- artifacts
- machine/session context when relevant

## Why this matters
Without durable workstream identity, EMA fractures into parallel universes and humans/agents lose continuity.
