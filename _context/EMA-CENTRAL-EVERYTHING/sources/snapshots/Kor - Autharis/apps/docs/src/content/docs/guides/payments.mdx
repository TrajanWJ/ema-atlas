---
title: Payments & payouts
description: How timesheets turn into invoices, and how invoices turn into payouts.
---

Payments in Autharis follow a single linear path: **timesheet → invoice →
paid → paid-out**. The end-to-end simulation lives in Lane E3 at
`autharis/components/payments/**`, `autharis/lib/payments/**`, and
`autharis/app/api/payments/**`.

## The lifecycle

1. **Timesheet submitted** by talent at the end of each week.
2. **Timesheet approved** by client (or auto-approved after the review
   window expires).
3. **Invoice issued** against the approved timesheet — line items derive
   from billable hours × negotiated rate, plus the platform fee.
4. **Invoice paid** by the client via the Stripe-style payment rail.
5. **Payout released** to talent on the configured cadence (typically
   weekly).

Every step is observable from both the client finance packet at
`/client-finance` and the talent earnings ledger at
`/talent/earnings/ledger`.

## Invoices

An invoice is a printable, immutable record. Once issued, its line items do
not change — any correction creates a credit note or a follow-up invoice.

Each invoice carries:

- A stable `id` (e.g. `inv-042`).
- The underlying `engagementId` and `timesheetId`.
- Line items, subtotal, platform fee, and total.
- A status in `draft | issued | paid | void`.
- The `paidAt` timestamp once settled.

## Payouts

A payout represents money moving from the Autharis ledger to the talent. It
references one or more paid invoices and appears on the talent earnings
ledger with a clear status: `scheduled`, `in-flight`, or `paid`.

The talent payout packet at `/talent/earnings/ledger` shows:

- Invoices rolled into the payout.
- Any holds or audit checkpoints that delayed release.
- The final paid amount after platform fees.

## Webhooks

Payment lifecycle events land at `POST /api/payments/webhook`. The shape is
Stripe-compatible so real rails can swap in without changing consumers.
Events fire on `invoice.issued`, `invoice.paid`, `payout.scheduled`, and
`payout.paid`.
