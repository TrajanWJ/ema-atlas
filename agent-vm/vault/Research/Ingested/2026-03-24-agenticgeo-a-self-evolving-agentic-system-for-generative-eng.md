## Summary

arXiv:2603.20213v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Generative search engines represent a transition from traditional ranking-based retrieval to Large Language Model (LLM)-based synthesis, transforming optimization goals from ranking prominence towards content inclusion. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), specifically, aims to maximize visibility and attribution in black-box summarized outputs by strategically manipulating source content.
