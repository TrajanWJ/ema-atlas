# ProMAS: Proactive Error Forecasting for Multi-Agent Systems Using Markov Transition Dynamics

## Summary

arXiv:2603.20260v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The integration of Large Language Models into Multi-Agent Systems (MAS) has enabled the so-lution of complex, long-horizon tasks through collaborative reasoning. However, this collec-tive intelligence is inherently fragile, as a single logical fallacy can rapidly propagate and lead to system-wide failure. Most current research re-lies on post-hoc failure analysis, thereby hinder-ing real-time intervention.

## Key Takeaways

- To address this, we propose PROMAS, a proactive framework utiliz-ing Markov transitions for predictive error anal-ysis.
- On the Who&When benchmark, PROMAS achieves 22.97% step-level accuracy while processing only 27% of reasoning logs.
