#### 🛠️ Overview

**obsidian-claude-code** is a workflow template designed for users who want to manage their knowledge effectively. This tool integrates seamless slash commands and structured formats to enhance your Obsidian experience. With this template, you can organize your ideas and notes more efficiently.
