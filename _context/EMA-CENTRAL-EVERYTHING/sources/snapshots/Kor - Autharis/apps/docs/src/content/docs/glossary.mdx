## Product terms

**Brief** — a draft or shared job description, usually created in the
marketing brief intake demo (`/brief`).

**Job request** — the structured, persisted version of a brief. Carries
title, category, client, description, hours-per-week, duration, timezone,
budget band, skills, industry, and status.

**Engagement** — an active agreement between a client and a specific
talent, scoped to a job request.

**Timesheet** — a weekly record of billable hours logged by talent against
an engagement.

**Invoice** — an immutable, printable record billed to the client against
one or more approved timesheets.

**Payout** — money moving from the Autharis ledger to talent, referencing
one or more paid invoices.

**Admin queue item** — an operator task awaiting review (activation
requests, disputes, matching audits, payout holds).

## Roles

**Client** — the buyer. Posts job requests, approves timesheets, pays
invoices.

**Talent** — the seller. Maintains a profile, accepts engagements, submits
timesheets, receives payouts.

**Admin** — the operator. Runs the activation queue, audits matching
runs, handles disputes, publishes weekly reports.

## Technical terms

**Surface** — one of the three top-level product UIs: marketing, client,
talent, admin.

**Lane** — a swarm coordination primitive. One agent, one holder, one
non-overlapping write scope. See [swarm process](/swarm/).

**Protected file** — a file inside the in-flight prototype lane that
non-reconciliation lanes must not edit.

**Deliverable** — a standalone workspace member (app, service, or
package) that ships on its own release cadence.

**Store** — the `services/api` data-access interface (`src/store/memory.ts`)
that lets an in-memory impl and a future Postgres impl share callers.
