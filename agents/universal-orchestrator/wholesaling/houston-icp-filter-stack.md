# Houston wholesaling ICP and filter-stack guide

## Objective
Build Houston-area PropStream lists that favor assignable off-market deals, not just distressed-looking properties.

## Houston ICP
The best near-term target is usually:
- single-family homes in working- and middle-class neighborhoods
- price points with active cash-buyer demand
- cosmetic-to-moderate rehab potential, not luxury teardown complexity
- owners with enough equity to discount
- situations with visible ownership friction or seller fatigue

## Property profile to prefer
- 3/1, 3/2, 4/2 bread-and-butter homes
- older housing stock where updates are common and repair scope is understandable
- neighborhoods where investor resales and rentals are normal
- properties with a clear buyer avatar: landlord, rehabber, or hybrid investor

## Property profile to avoid by default
- luxury retail-sensitive areas
- severe flood-risk pockets unless buyer demand is proven
- highly customized homes with uncertain exit
- very low spread opportunities where assignment margin is fragile
- heavy title / legal complexity unless routed to specialist workflow

## Recommended filter stack order

### Layer 1: Market and geography
- Houston core target zones first
- prioritize zip codes / micro-markets with known cash-buyer activity
- avoid citywide exports without submarket logic

### Layer 2: Asset class
- single-family residential first
- optionally small multifamily only where buyer list supports it

### Layer 3: Owner and equity foundation
- owner equity floor appropriate for wholesale discounting
- longer ownership duration preferred
- absentee owner useful but not required if other signals are strong

### Layer 4: Motivation stack
Best combinations:
- absentee + high equity + long ownership
- vacancy proxy + high equity
- tax delinquent + absentee
- tired landlord + older property + long hold period
- inherited / estate proxy + deferred maintenance indicators
- pre-foreclosure + sufficient equity + realistic disposition zone

### Layer 5: Practical outreach viability
- clean mailing and owner fields
- skip-traceable identity
- not recently sold or actively retail-listed
- not obviously outside current buyer appetite

## Suggested Houston cohort templates

### Cohort A: Bread-and-butter absentee distress
- single-family
- non-owner occupied
- equity above threshold
- ownership 8+ years
- older build year
- one or more distress overlays
Use for: cold call, SMS, direct mail

### Cohort B: Tired landlord
- absentee owner
- likely rental corridor
- ownership 10+ years
- no recent listing
- moderate/high equity
Use for: empathetic landlord relief messaging

### Cohort C: Equity-rich inherited / legacy owner
- long ownership
- estate / inherited proxy when available
- property age suggests deferred updates
- solid buyer demand area
Use for: softer consultative outreach

### Cohort D: Fast-moving distress
- pre-foreclosure or similar urgency signal
- enough equity to solve problem
- disposition viability confirmed quickly
Use for: rapid response and fast underwriting

## Field guidance for the system
Each exported lead should retain:
- market cohort name
- zip / submarket
- matched filters
- motivation reason codes
- priority tier
- recommended script angle
- underwriting watch-outs

## KPI interpretation for Houston
A Houston list is strong if it produces:
- good contact rate without burning data spend
- meaningful conversation rate from first touches
- seller pain points that match the reason codes
- deals that survive conservative underwriting
- repeatable assignment spreads in the same cohort

## Simple rule
If the list cannot answer “why would this Houston owner realistically sell at a discount now?”, it is not ready for campaign launch.
