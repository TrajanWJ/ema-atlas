# Design Inspiration — Branding and Assets

Curated catalog of design inspiration sites focused on branding, visual identity, and UI assets. Each entry researched hands-on, not from descriptions.

---

## 1. Rebrand Gallery

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **URL** | https://www.rebrand.gallery |
| **What it is** | Curated gallery of brand identity redesigns, visual identity systems, and rebrand videos |
| **Run by** | [Sahkyo Studio](https://sahkyo.com) (design studio), founded July 2023 by Andreas Storm's tweet inspiration |
| **Pricing** | **Freemium** — free account shows ~21 rebrands before hitting a sign-up wall. **Pro** at €1.67/month (billed yearly, ~€20/year) with 7-day free trial. Free tier has preview limits; Pro unlocks full access, filters, search, and dark mode |
| **Community** | 30,000+ registered designers, 22K+ followers on X/Twitter (@rebrandgallery) |

### Content Organization

Three main sections accessible from top nav:

- **Intros** (`/`) — The flagship gallery. Each entry is a brand with its rebrand video, logo, and visual identity breakdown. Cards show brand logo + name, link to dedicated pages
- **Bentos** (`/bentos`) — Brand bento grid layouts — visual overviews of design systems arranged in bento-style grids. Think mood-board-meets-brand-guidelines snapshots
- **Products** (`/products`) — Product feature videos and experience introductions (newer section, expanding the scope beyond pure rebranding)

**Pro-only filtering** (not available on free tier):
- By style, color, industry, and studio
- Natural language search (e.g., "elegant artisanal illustration")
- Search by industry, agency, or tags

### Volume of Content

Visible on the free homepage: ~21 rebrands before the sign-up wall kicks in. Behind the wall, the full gallery is larger (exact count not publicly disclosed, but they've been curating since July 2023 with consistent additions). Based on the URL slugs and regular posting cadence, estimate **150–300+ rebrands** in the gallery.

### Notable Brands Featured (sampled from homepage)

- **Warp** — developer tools rebrand
- **Cloudflare** — major tech infrastructure rebrand
- **Electronic Arts (EA)** — gaming giant identity refresh
- **Domino's** — consumer brand evolution
- **Superhuman** — email client rebrand
- **Transavia** — airline identity
- **Joby Aviation** — eVTOL/aviation startup
- **Toca Boca** — kids' gaming brand
- **Yes! Apples** — food/agriculture brand
- **Mapfre** — insurance company
- **BBH** — advertising agency
- **Maxon** — 3D software (Cinema 4D parent)

The mix spans tech, consumer goods, airlines, gaming, fintech, crypto (ZK Nation, Aptos, Chaos Labs), and traditional industries. Not just Silicon Valley — genuinely diverse.

### Submissions

Studios and freelancers can submit rebrands for **€80**, which includes:
- A bespoke brand bento element crafted from provided assets
- Permanent gallery placement
- Promotional feature shared with their 22K+ X/Twitter following

### Quality Assessment

**Verdict: Genuinely high quality — not aggregation spam.**

- Each entry is hand-curated by a design studio (Sahkyo), not auto-scraped
- Focus on rebrand *videos* is unique — most design galleries show static screenshots. The video-first approach shows motion design, transitions, and brand storytelling
- The bento grid feature is a distinctive format you don't see elsewhere
- The paid submission model means studios self-select quality work
- The paywall at ~21 items is aggressive for discovery, but the Pro price (€20/year) is very accessible
- Clean, well-designed site that practices what it preaches

### Patterns & Standout Observations

- **Video-first** differentiator — most brand galleries are static image collections. Rebrand leads with brand introduction videos, which capture motion design and brand storytelling much better
- **Bento grids** as a format — popularized in tech (Apple-style grid layouts), applied here to brand identity systems as visual summaries
- **Crypto/Web3 brands** well represented (Aptos, ZK Nation, Chaos Labs, Arrakis) — often underserved in traditional design galleries
- **Strong editorial curation** — not a firehose; each entry feels intentionally chosen

### Competitors / Similar Sites

- [Brand New (underconsideration.com)](https://www.underconsideration.com/brandnew/) — the OG rebrand critique blog, but editorial/text-heavy vs. visual gallery
- [Logggos](https://www.logggos.club/) — logo-focused inspiration
- [Brandpad](https://brandpad.io/gallery) — brand guideline showcases
- [Dribbble](https://dribbble.com) / [Behance](https://behance.net) — broader design showcases with brand identity sections
- [Mobbin](https://mobbin.com) — UI/UX focused, not branding specifically

## 2. Hugeicons

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **URL** | https://hugeicons.com |
| **What it is** | Massive icon library with 51,000+ icons across 10 styles and 59 categories, built for UI design and development |
| **Run by** | Masum Parvej (creator), under [Halal Lab](https://halallab.co/) |
| **Pricing** | **Freemium** — 5,100+ stroke rounded icons free. **Pro** at $99/year for full 51,000+ library with all styles |
| **Users** | 300,000+ creatives worldwide (per their docs) |

### Content Organization

**59 categories** spanning the full UI icon spectrum. Browsable at `/icons`.

**10 icon styles** (Pro unlocks all; free = Stroke Rounded only):
1. Stroke Rounded
2. Stroke Sharp
3. Solid Rounded
4. Solid Sharp
5. Bulk Rounded
6. Bulk Sharp
7. Duotone Rounded
8. Duotone Sharp
9. Twotone Rounded
10. Twotone Sharp

Each icon is available in all 10 styles, so the 51,000+ figure = ~5,100 unique icons × 10 style variants.

### Volume

- **5,100+ unique icons** (base set)
- **51,000+ total** when counting all 10 style variants
- **59 categories** of organization
- Actively maintained with new icons added

### Notable Features

**For Designers:**
- Figma file (full library)
- Figma plugin (search + insert directly)
- Framer plugin
- Iconjar file
- SVG downloads
- **AI-powered search** (Pro only) — find icons by natural language description

**For Developers:**
- **Framework packages:** React, Vue, Angular, Svelte, React Native, Flutter
- **CMS:** WordPress plugin
- **Developer tools:** VS Code extension, web fonts, SVG files
- **3rd-party integrations:** Blade (Laravel), Django
- **MCP Server** — AI assistant integration via Model Context Protocol (notable for AI agent workflows)
- NPM registry hosting
- Tree-shaking support
- TypeScript compatible
- CDN webfonts for icon sets

**Web App:**
- Browse all 51,000+ icons at `/icons`
- Search, preview, copy icon name/format, download SVGs
- Filter by style and type

**Pro Plan Specifics ($99/year):**
- 1 seat license key (team seats available)
- 300K pageviews included
- 2GB bandwidth included
- 6 project icon sets
- CDN webfonts
- Overage: $1/GB extra bandwidth, $3/300K extra pageviews
- 30-day money-back guarantee

### Quality Assessment

**Verdict: Legitimate, high-quality professional icon library — a serious Lucide/Phosphor/Heroicons competitor.**

- Icons are genuinely well-crafted — consistent stroke widths, balanced proportions, clean geometry
- 10 style variants per icon is a real differentiator (most libraries offer 2-3 styles max)
- Framework support is comprehensive — not just React, but Vue, Angular, Svelte, Flutter, React Native
- The MCP server integration is forward-thinking for AI-assisted development
- Performance-focused: tree-shaking, lightweight SVGs, TypeScript support
- Testimonials from recognizable names (Untitled UI, Prototypr, Layers)
- The free tier (5,100 icons, stroke rounded only) is genuinely usable, not a crippled demo
- $99/year is competitive — Phosphor is free but has fewer styles; Font Awesome Pro is similar price with less variety

### Patterns & Standout Observations

- **MCP Server support** — unique among icon libraries. Allows AI coding assistants to search and use icons programmatically
- **Bandwidth metering on Pro** — unusual for an icon library. Most competitors don't meter usage. The 300K pageview + 2GB bandwidth limits could be a concern for high-traffic apps
- **Style depth** — 10 styles is genuinely best-in-class. Most competitors top out at filled/outlined/duotone
- **Strong documentation** — dedicated docs site (docs.hugeicons.com) with integration guides per framework
- **Creator-led** — Masum's personal guarantee letter on the pricing page adds a personal touch unusual for a design tool

### Competitors / Similar Sites

- **[Lucide](https://lucide.dev)** — open-source, ~1,500 icons, 1 style. Free. The default for many React projects
- **[Phosphor Icons](https://phosphoricons.com)** — 7,488 icons, 6 styles. Free/open-source. Closest competitor in style variety
- **[Heroicons](https://heroicons.com)** — by Tailwind Labs, ~300 icons, 3 styles. Free. Minimal but high quality
- **[Font Awesome](https://fontawesome.com)** — ~26,000 icons, Pro tier. The legacy giant, but aging design language
- **[Tabler Icons](https://tabler.io/icons)** — 5,600+ icons, stroke style. Free/open-source
- **[Remix Icon](https://remixicon.com)** — 2,800+ icons, neutral style. Free
- **[Iconoir](https://iconoir.com)** — 1,500+ icons, open-source. Minimal
- **[Bootstrap Icons](https://icons.getbootstrap.com)** — 2,000+ icons, free. Bootstrap ecosystem

## When to Use Each

- **Rebrand Gallery** → Starting a branding project. Need to study how other companies handle identity redesigns. Want to see motion design and brand system presentations. Client pitch references.
- **Hugeicons** → Building a product UI. Need a comprehensive, consistent icon set that works across React/Vue/Flutter/etc. Want style flexibility (stroke, solid, duotone, bulk, twotone) without switching libraries.
