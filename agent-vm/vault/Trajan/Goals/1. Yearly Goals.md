## NOT Doing This Year

Equally important — what gets a deliberate NO:

1. **No more new project starts without shipping existing ones.** The portfolio ([[Trajan's Projects]]) is already 9+ active projects. Ship before starting.
2. **No building systems as procrastination.** If it doesn't lead to revenue or clients within 30 days, it waits.
3. **No perfectionism on launches.** Ship ugly, get feedback, iterate. ExecuDeck at 70% and not launched is worse than ExecuDeck at 80% and live.
