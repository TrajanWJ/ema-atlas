# GSI Agent: Domain Knowledge Enhancement for Large Language Models in Green Stormwater Infrastructure

## Summary

arXiv:2603.15643v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Green Stormwater Infrastructure (GSI) systems, such as permeable pavement, rain gardens, and bioretention facilities, require continuous inspection and maintenance to ensure long-term performance. However, domain knowledge about GSI is often scattered across municipal manuals, regulatory documents, and inspection forms. As a result, non-expert users and maintenance staff may struggle to obtain reliable and actionable guidance from field observations.

## Key Takeaways

- In this paper, we propose GSI Agent, a domain-enhanced LLM framework designed to improve performance in GSI-related tasks.
- Experimental results show that our framework significantly improves domain-specific performance while maintaining general knowledge capability.
